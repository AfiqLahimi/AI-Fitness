anatomy.glb — 3D human muscle and bone model used for muscle highlighting.

Derived from:
  "Z-Anatomy - The libre 3D atlas of anatomy" — CC BY-SA 4.0
  https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
which is based on:
  "BodyParts3D - The Database Center for Life Science" — CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan
  https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/

Changes: muscles (skeletal muscular system) and major bones were extracted, reduced in
polygon count and compressed for the web (scripts/anatomy/). Materials were removed.
